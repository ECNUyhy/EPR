# Documents

`EPR-appendix.pdf`: the online appendix of the paper. It gives the system model, the assumptions A1-A9 that the
proofs use, and the full proofs of every lemma, theorem and proposition (the guarded-delta and guarded-program
soundness lemmas with the gas argument, escrow safety, serial equivalence, the snapshot rule for queries,
determinism across organizations, and per-transaction maximality), followed by the references. Results of the
paper keep their numbers.
